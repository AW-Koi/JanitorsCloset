# Design notes to discuss

Raised on 2026-10-25 while reading the reviews. Still open; decide once all the reports are read.

## The closet as a resupply loop

The janitor has to come back to the closet to keep doing the job well. The closet is the hub of pillar 4, not just storage.

- **Glittervacuum:** it fills up with toxic waste packs as it cleans, and only a dedicated support building can empty it. That building is probably a new 1x1 glittervacuum dock in the closet, so returning to the closet is required to keep the vacuum working.
  - Once the waste packs are out, they're ordinary items the player manages, the same as vanilla wastepacks: store them, dump them or launch them.
  - Still open: the pack capacity, what happens when the vacuum is full, and whether the dock needs power.
- **Other eras:** the loop should carry across every tech level, not just spacer. Each era needs its own equivalent, still to be worked out.
- **Mop bucket:** fill it from a water source. Dubs Bad Hygiene integration could supply the water. This needs a fallback for colonies without the mod, which is also still to be worked out.
