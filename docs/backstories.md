# Janitor backstories

The four backstories in `mod/Common/Defs/BackstoryDefs/Backstories_Janitor.xml`, with vanilla's two cleaning backstories for comparison. Each mod backstory requires the pawn to be capable of cleaning.

## Janitor's Closet

### Janitor's kid (childhood)

- **Short title:** mop kid
- **Spawns for:** Offworld, Outlander
- **Skills:** Construction +1, Social +1

> [PAWN_nameDef] spent [PAWN_possessive] childhood trailing a parent through the corridors of an orbital habitat after hours, carrying the bucket.
>
> [PAWN_pronoun] learned which panels hid the best shortcuts, and that nobody notices a clean floor until it isn't.

### Custodian (adulthood)

- **Short title:** custodian
- **Spawns for:** Offworld, Outlander
- **Skills:** Construction +3, Social +1

> [PAWN_nameDef] kept a glitterworld academy spotless for two decades. Students came and went; the floors stayed immaculate.
>
> [PAWN_pronoun] can fix a jammed door, calm a flooded lavatory and find anything anyone ever lost. [PAWN_pronoun] has opinions about mud.

### Sanitation worker (adulthood)

- **Short title:** sanitation
- **Spawns for:** Offworld, Outlander, Pirate
- **Skills:** Melee +2, Construction +2

> [PAWN_nameDef] worked the night shift in the waste tunnels beneath an urbworld megacity, where the rats were the size of dogs and the dogs were worse.
>
> [PAWN_pronoun] can stomach any mess, and keeps a heavy tool within reach.

### Latrine keeper (adulthood)

- **Short title:** keeper
- **Spawns for:** Tribal
- **Skills:** Plants +2, Melee +1

> [PAWN_nameDef]'s tribe gave [PAWN_objective] the job nobody wanted: digging the latrine trenches, hauling the night soil to the fields, and sweeping the longhouse floor before the elders woke.
>
> [PAWN_pronoun] knows which plants grow best on what [PAWN_pronoun] carried, and nobody argues with someone holding the dung shovel.

## Vanilla

### Starship janitor (adulthood, `StarshipJanitor33`)

- **Short title:** janitor
- **Spawns for:** Offworld, ImperialCommon, Researcher
- **Skills:** Construction +2, Crafting +3
- **Requires:** dumb and skilled manual labour

> While other passengers pass the years between star systems in cryptosleep sarcophagi, [PAWN_nameDef] had to wake up at periodic intervals to perform inspections, check the navigation systems, and to oil the mechanoids.

### Coliseum cleaner (childhood, `ColiseumCleaner54`)

- **Short title:** cleaner
- **Skills:** Intellectual +4, Social +2, Crafting +1
- **Disables:** caring, firefighting, mining

## Coverage

Vanilla's starship janitor covers the Offworld spaceship cleaner, so the mod adds no ship backstory. The latrine keeper gives tribal starts a janitor, and the custodian and sanitation worker cover industrial and urban worlds.
